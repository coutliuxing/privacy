牛马办公室 隐私政策

应用包名：com.cbgames.wormhunt ｜ 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

中文English

《牛马办公室》（下称"本游戏"）是一款单机休闲益智游戏。本政策说明本游戏会用到哪些信息、为什么用、你有哪些选择。我们尽量把话说直白。

1. 我们自己不收集个人信息

本游戏没有账号系统，不要求你注册、登录或填写任何个人资料。游戏进度、金币、设置等存在你的设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器（我们也没有服务器）。卸载游戏即删除这些本地数据。

2. 第三方服务会处理的信息

为了展示广告和处理购买，本游戏接入了以下第三方服务。它们按各自的隐私政策处理信息，我们不会收到你的个人信息。

3. 广告与你的选择

首次打开游戏时会弹出隐私提示，你可以选择：

  • 同意并开始：广告方可按兴趣展示个性化广告；
  • 仅必要广告：只展示非个性化广告，广告方不会用你的数据做兴趣推断。

两种选择都不影响游戏内容。你也可以在手机系统设置里重置或关闭广告标识符（Android："设置 → Google → 广告"）。购买"去广告"商品后，插屏和横幅广告不再出现。

4. 通知

本游戏只发本地提醒（例如每日奖励可以领了），不使用推送服务器。Android 13 及以上会先征求你的同意，你可以随时在系统设置里关闭。

5. 权限

本游戏申请的权限：网络（加载广告、完成购买）、通知（本地提醒）、广告标识符（广告方使用）、Google Play 结算。本游戏不申请位置、通讯录、相机、麦克风、存储读取等权限。

6. 儿童

本游戏面向 13 岁以上用户，不是专为儿童设计的。我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息；若你认为儿童向我们提供了信息，请联系我们删除。

7. 你的权利

你可以随时选择非个性化广告、重置广告标识符或卸载游戏。对于广告方处理的信息，可按上表链接向其行使访问、删除等权利。欧洲经济区、英国、加州等地区用户依当地法律享有的权利同样适用。

8. 本政策的更新

若本游戏接入新的服务（例如崩溃上报），我们会在此页面更新并修改生效日期。重大变更会在游戏内再次提示。

Privacy Policy

App: 牛马办公室 (Worm Hunt) ｜ Package: com.cbgames.wormhunt ｜ Effective: 21 October 2026

This is a single-player casual puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have.

1. We do not collect personal information ourselves

The game has no accounts and never asks you to register, sign in or enter personal details. Progress, coins and settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us (we run no servers). Uninstalling the game deletes this local data.

2. Information handled by third-party services

3. Ads and your choices

On first launch the game shows a privacy notice with two options:

  • Agree and start: the ad provider may show personalised ads based on interests;
  • Essential ads only: only non-personalised ads are shown and your data is not used for interest profiling.

Neither choice changes the game itself. You can also reset or disable your advertising identifier in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). After buying the "remove ads" product, interstitial and banner ads no longer appear.

4. Notifications

The game only schedules local reminders (for example, that a daily reward is ready); no push server is used. Android 13 and later ask for your permission first, and you can turn notifications off in system settings at any time.

5. Permissions

The game requests: network access (ads and purchases), notifications (local reminders), the advertising identifier (used by the ad provider) and Google Play Billing. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

6. Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can switch to non-personalised ads, reset your advertising identifier or uninstall the game at any time. For information handled by the ad provider, use the links above to exercise access, deletion and similar rights. Rights granted by local law in the EEA, the UK, California and elsewhere apply as well.

8. Changes

If the game adds a new service (for example crash reporting), this page will be updated with a new effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the game again.
